Visual designer working across branding, fashion, and advertising.

I’m Joel Cormack, a Canadian visual designer based in Los Angeles. I create identities, apparel graphics, and digital experiences, with an interest in how brands move between culture, image, and everyday life.

At SixTwentySix, I design director treatments, pitch decks, and brand systems for national campaigns, translating creative ideas into a clear visual direction.

Previously, I worked at Otis Design Lab and earned my BFA in Graphic Design from Otis College of Art and Design. Before moving to LA, I studied political science and art history at the University of Alberta.
